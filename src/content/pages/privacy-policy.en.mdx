## 2. Personal Information We Collect & Consent Requirements

To deliver our AI video generation services, we collect:

1. **Account Credentials**: Name, email address, password (securely hashed), and profile images, including OAuth identifiers when signing in through Google or GitHub.
2. **Prompts & Creative Assets**: Prompts, lyrics, reference portraits, audio tracks, and output videos generated through our AI pipeline.
3. **Billing and Transaction Records**: Transaction amounts, currency, subscription status, and purchase dates. We do **not** collect or store sensitive payment card numbers on our servers (see Section 5).
4. **Technical & Telemetry Data**: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, session duration, and feature interactions used for fraud prevention and performance monitoring.
5. **Cookies & Local Storage**: Session tokens and UI preferences necessary to maintain authenticated sessions and language selection.
6. **Third-Party Portrait Consent Warranty (Important)**: When you upload photos containing third parties (such as friends, colleagues, or other real individuals) for dual rap battles or animation, **you warrant and represent that you have obtained express, informed legal consent from the portrayed individuals**. Using another person's likeness without authorization for defamation, non-consensual deepfakes, or harassment is strictly prohibited.

## 4. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We **do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information** to third parties, as defined under GDPR, CCPA, and relevant data protection laws. User-submitted generation prompts and private outputs are not used to train public machine learning models without your explicit consent.

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers & Model Inference Pipelines

We collaborate with trusted infrastructure partners to operate our platform:

- **Payment Processing (Waffo Pancake)**: All payment card processing, checkout flows, and recurring renewals are handled exclusively by our authorized Merchant of Record partner, **Waffo Pancake** ([https://pancake.waffo.ai](https://pancake.waffo.ai)), in full compliance with **PCI-DSS Level 1** security standards. Sensitive card numbers and CVV codes are processed solely by Waffo Pancake and are never stored on our servers.
- **Content Safety & Prompt Screening (Waffo Prompt Screening Gateway)**: Prompts and creative text inputs are scanned prior to generation dispatch via automated content safety APIs to detect and block CSAM, NCII, hate speech, and violent content in strict accordance with our [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use-policy).
- **AI Compute & Model Inference (Kling / Wan / Suno APIs)**: Video diffusion and audio generation run on dedicated, access-isolated cloud GPU infrastructure. All upstream API transmissions operate under strict **Zero-Data Retention agreements**; third-party model providers process tasks ephemerally and do not persist facial biometrics or use your assets to train foundation models.

## 7. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable privacy legislation (including GDPR and CCPA), you are entitled to:

- **Access & Portability**: Request a copy of your personal data;
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate or outdated profile details;
- **Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)**: Request the erasure of your personal data;
- **Restrict or Object**: Restrict processing or object to legitimate-interest processing;
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent previously granted for specific data processing.

To exercise these rights, submit your request to **support@airap-video.net**. We process all verified requests within **thirty (30) calendar days**.

## 8. Protection of Minors

Airap Video is intended solely for users aged **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has registered an account, contact us immediately at **support@airap-video.net** to have the account and data removed.
