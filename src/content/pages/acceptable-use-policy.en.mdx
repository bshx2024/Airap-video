## 1. Scope & Core Mission

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and standards governing the creation, upload, generation, and distribution of artificial intelligence media on **Airap Video** ([https://airap-video.net](https://airap-video.net)), operated by **Hua Peng**.

Our platform empowers musicians, content creators, and storytellers to produce AI rap battles, hip-hop music, and stylized visual art. We maintain zero tolerance for unlawful exploitation, harm, or abuse.

By accessing the Service, you agree to comply with this Policy in full, alongside our [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## 2. Prohibited Content Categories

You may not input, upload, synthesize, download, or distribute any content falling under the following categories:

### 2.1 Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM / CSAE)
- Any media depicting, encouraging, or facilitating sexual abuse or exploitation of minors.
- **Enforcement**: Absolute zero tolerance. Immediate permanent ban, immediate reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant criminal authorities.

### 2.2 Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII) & Explicit Adult Content
- Nudity, pornographic media, sexual acts, erotic fetishes, or sexually suggestive depictions of individuals without their explicit written consent.
- Generating sexualized or intimate media using photos of real living individuals (celebrities, acquaintances, or private citizens).

### 2.3 Extreme Violence, Gore & Terrorist Material
- Depictions of graphic bloodshed, torture, severe mutilation, mass casualty events, or animal cruelty.
- Propaganda promoting terrorist organizations, violent extremism, or instructions on constructing weapons.
- Content encouraging suicide, self-mutilation, or eating disorders.

### 2.4 Hate Speech, Discrimination & Severe Harassment
- Content attacking, dehumanizing, or inciting hatred against protected groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religious affiliation, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, or serious disability.
- Doxxing, targeted online bullying, defamation, blackmail, or cyberstalking.

### 2.5 Deceptive Deepfakes & Impersonation
- Creating misleading or realistic synthetic audio/video depicting real people saying or doing things they never did, used for political disinformation, public fraud, stock manipulation, or character assassination.
- Impersonating corporate, legal, or government entities.

### 2.6 Intellectual Property & Publicity Rights Infringement
- Utilizing trademarked logos, commercial cartoon mascots, or proprietary character designs to pass off counterfeit merchandise or deceive consumers.
- Mass scraping or direct copyright infringement of proprietary media assets.

### 2.7 System Abuse, Cyberattacks & Adversarial Probing
- Executing prompt injection, jailbreaking techniques, or reverse-engineering platform safety layers.
- Operating bots, scrapers, or scripts designed to exhaust compute clusters or bypass credit metering.

## 3. Graduated Enforcement Actions & Timelines (违规处置机制与时限)

We enforce violations swiftly and systematically based on severity:

| Violation Tier | Description / Examples | Immediate Enforcement Action | Effective Timeline | Financial Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1 (Severe / Unlawful)** | CSAM, NCII, Terrorism, Malicious Deepfakes, Criminal Fraud | Immediate permanent account termination, permanent IP/device block, asset deletion, law enforcement referral | **Within 1 hour** upon detection | **All remaining credits cleared and forfeited; strictly 0% refund** |

Under no circumstances are credits, subscription payments, or one-time fees refunded to accounts that are suspended or terminated for violating this Acceptable Use Policy.

- **Dedicated Abuse Email**: **`report@airap-video.net`**

  - Content takedown & user ban: **within twenty-four (24) hours**.
- **Standard Reports (Copyright, Defamation, Impersonation)**:
  - First acknowledgment: **within twenty-four (24) hours**;
  - Investigation & final resolution: **within three (3) to five (5) business days**.

## 5. Platform Content Moderation Architecture (平台审核机制说明)

Airap Video implements a robust, multi-stage content safety architecture:

1. **Pre-Generation Prompt Screening (前置审核)**:
   - All text inputs (rap topics, lyric prompts, visual styles) are screened in real-time using the **Waffo Prompt Screening API** prior to reaching GPU compute nodes.
   - Any prompt receiving an action verdict of `block` or `review` is rejected at the API perimeter, protecting infrastructure and preventing illicit media generation.
2. **Post-Generation Output Verification (后置审核)**:
   - Rendered video frames and generated images pass through computer vision safety filters and perceptual hashing to detect unintended NSFW, gore, or CSAM indicators before delivery.
3. **Behavioral Anomaly Monitoring**:
   - Automated rate-limiters and fraud detection algorithms monitor for prompt enumeration, script attacks, and adversarial jailbreaks.
4. **Human Review & Appeals**:
   - Complex reports and borderline edge cases are manually evaluated by our Trust & Safety compliance team.
